Data Scientist - Machine Learning

About the role
We are looking for a Data Scientist to build and deploy machine learning models that power our product recommendations and demand forecasting.

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience in data science or machine learning roles
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics or a related field
- Strong programming skills in Python (pandas, numpy, scikit-learn)
- Solid SQL skills for data extraction and analysis
- Good understanding of statistics, feature engineering and model evaluation
- Experience with data visualization and communicating insights to stakeholders

Nice to have
- Deep learning experience with TensorFlow or PyTorch
- NLP experience
- Cloud experience (AWS) and Docker
- Experience with MLOps practices such as MLflow
